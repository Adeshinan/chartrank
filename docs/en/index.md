# ChartRank

**Suggests and explains the right chart for your data and intent.**

Give ChartRank one or two columns and an intent. It proposes 3 to 5 charts drawn
on your own data, marks the recommended one with ★, explains each with numbers
computed from your data, and gives you the image or a standalone Python script
to reproduce it. Deterministic, local, no AI.
